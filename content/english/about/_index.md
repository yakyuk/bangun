#### Our Story

##### _Started with a Helping Hand, Built on Trust_

The story of Yakyuk Bangun began during the challenging early days of the COVID-19 pandemic. It all started unexpectedly when a close friend asked for help finding a place to live. What seemed like a simple favor soon turned into something much bigger.

After successfully finding the right home, we were entrusted once again—this time to renovate and improve the property. That first project, carried out with dedication and sincerity, became the foundation for everything that followed.

Through word-of-mouth recommendations and the trust of satisfied clients, Yakyuk Bangun continued to grow. Today, we have evolved into a trusted construction and renovation partner, helping homeowners bring their dream spaces to life.

This journey is shared firsthand by the founder and owner of Yakyuk Bangun, Mr. Sholakhuddin.

#### Company Overview

Yakyuk Bangun is a construction, renovation, and handyman service contractor serving Sleman and the surrounding areas. We handle a wide range of building projects, from home repairs and fence construction to cost-effective budgeting and project planning services.

We are committed to helping clients create comfortable, functional, and long-lasting properties through our five core services:

- **New Construction** – Complete building projects from the ground up, supported by careful planning and quality workmanship.
- **Renovation** – Transform outdated spaces into fresh, functional, and visually appealing environments tailored to your needs.
- **Repair & Maintenance** – Fast and reliable solutions for minor issues as well as major structural repairs.
- **Steel & Aluminum Works** – Precision fabrication and installation of steel structures, gates, container booths, canopies, and aluminum frames.
- **Electrical Installation** – Safe and professional electrical installation, maintenance, and troubleshooting services.

At Yakyuk Bangun, we believe that every project deserves attention to detail, honest workmanship, and a commitment to customer satisfaction. Whether you are building a new home, renovating an existing property, or carrying out essential repairs, we are ready to be your trusted construction partner.
